Cookie policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page lists everything Semaphore Lab stores in your browser. There are no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no third-party cookies.

Cookies

Set only when you sign in. They are strictly necessary for the account to work, so they do not need consent. Page scripts cannot read them (HttpOnly), they are sent only over HTTPS and only to our own server addresses under /api.

NamePurposeKept for
sl_atProves you are signed in1 hour
sl_rtRenews the sign-in without asking for your password again30 days, or until you sign out

Browser storage

These items stay on your device and are never sent to us.

NamePurposeKept for
semlab-themeYour colour theme (light or dark)Until you change it or clear site data
semlab-consentYour choice in the cookie bannerUntil you change it or clear site data
semlab-onboardedThat you finished or skipped the introductionUntil you clear site data

Optional error reports

Error reports are not cookies, but they are optional, so they are part of the same choice. If you allow them, the page sends a short anonymous report when something breaks. The privacy policy describes exactly what is in one.

Changing your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the main site at any time. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; you will then be signed out.
