Refund policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Semaphore Lab is completely free. We never ask for payment, card details or bank details. There are no subscriptions, upgrades, in-app purchases or hidden fees, so there is nothing to refund.

If anyone asks you to pay for Semaphore Lab or for access to it, that request does not come from us. Please ignore it.

If paid features are ever introduced, this policy will be updated first, prices will be shown clearly before any purchase, and nothing will be charged without your explicit agreement.

Semaphore Lab is a free student Operating Systems project run by Sundaresan D, India. It is not a company and sells nothing.
