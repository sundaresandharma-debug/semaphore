Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Semaphore Lab handles, why, and what you can do about it. Semaphore Lab is an educational website that runs a semaphore written in Python and shows what the threads do.

Who is responsible

The website is run by Sundaresan D, an individual based in India, as a student project. For anything about your data, including complaints (grievances), write to [your-email].

The short version

  • You can use every demonstration, the Playground, the tests and the source viewer without an account. In that case we store nothing that identifies you.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address, your password in hashed form, and the results you choose to save.
  • There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. We never sell or share data for marketing.

What we collect, and why

When you use the site without an account

Your browser sends the settings of a demonstration or your Playground clicks to our server, which runs the Python code and sends back the result. These requests are not stored by us.

To protect the service from overload, the server counts requests per visitor for one minute. It does this with a scrambled form of your IP address (hashed with a random key that is replaced whenever the server restarts). It is kept in memory only and is never written to disk.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes your IP address and basic request details (time, address requested, browser type) to deliver the site and to keep it secure, and keeps them in its logs for a limited period.

When you create an account

  • Email address: to sign you in, to confirm the account and to send password reset links. We do not send newsletters.
  • Password: stored only as a secure hash by our account provider. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Consent record: that you accepted these terms and confirmed you are 18 or older, with the date.
  • Saved results: only the ones you press Save this result for. Each one holds the demonstration name, its settings, the outcome, how long it took and when it was saved.

We use this data only to provide the account features you asked for (performance of a contract, and your consent).

Error reports, only if you allow them

If you choose Allow error reports in the cookie banner, the page sends a short report when something breaks: the error message, the file and line where it happened and which section was open. It does not include your name, email, account or any content you typed. Reports appear in our server logs at Vercel. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

Stored in your browser

Your colour theme, your cookie choice and whether you finished the introduction are saved in your own browser and are never sent to us. Signing in sets two cookies. The cookie policy lists every item.

How long we keep it

  • Account data and saved results: until you delete them or your account.
  • Rate-limit counters: one minute, in memory only.
  • Hosting logs and error reports: for the limited period Vercel keeps logs.
  • Database backups: encrypted copies kept for up to 14 days, after which deleted data is gone from backups too.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR), you can ask to see, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, object to processing, receive a copy of your data, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to a data protection authority.

  • Delete everything yourself: on the Account page, Delete my account immediately and permanently removes your account and every saved result. Delete all saved results removes only the results.
  • Ask us: email [your-email] from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days, and sooner where the law requires.

Children

Accounts are only for people aged 18 or older. Under India's data protection law, a person under 18 is a child, and their data may only be processed with verifiable parental consent, which this site does not collect. Younger visitors can use every demonstration without an account, and then nothing about them is stored. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Sign-in cookies cannot be read by page scripts. Database keys stay on the server and are never sent to your browser. The database only lets each account read and delete its own results (row level security). Requests are rate limited, and backups are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how account data is used, we will say so on the site before it takes effect.
