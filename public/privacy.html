Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Semaphore Lab handles, why, and what you can do about it. Semaphore Lab is an educational website that runs a semaphore written in Python and shows what the threads do.

Who is responsible

The website is run by Sundaresan D, an individual based in India, as a student project.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that ask for your name or email address.
  • The site stores nothing that identifies you.
  • There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. We never sell or share data.

What happens when you use the site

Your browser sends the settings of a demonstration or your Playground clicks to our server, which runs the Python code and sends back the result. These requests are not stored by us.

To protect the service from overload, the server counts requests per visitor for one minute. It does this with a scrambled form of your IP address (hashed with a random key that is replaced whenever the server restarts). It is kept in memory only and is never written to disk.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes your IP address and basic request details (time, address requested, browser type) to deliver the site and to keep it secure, and keeps them in its logs for a limited period.

Error reports, only if you allow them

If you choose Allow error reports in the cookie notice, the page sends a short report when something breaks: the error message, the file and line where it happened and which section was open. It contains nothing that identifies you. Reports appear in our server logs at Vercel. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

Stored in your browser

Your colour theme, your cookie choice and whether you finished the introduction are saved in your own browser and are never sent to us. The cookie policy lists each item.

Vercel may process data outside India. It acts on our instructions and protects it under its own security and data protection terms.

How long it is kept

  • Rate-limit counters: one minute, in memory only.
  • Hosting logs and error reports: for the limited period Vercel keeps logs.
  • Items in your browser: until you clear your site data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR), you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, withdraw consent and complain to a data protection authority. Because the site keeps no record that identifies you, there is usually nothing to return or delete.

To remove what is stored in your browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

The site collects no personal data from anyone, so it can be used at any age. It does not ask for a name, email address or age.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, loads no third-party code, limits how many requests each visitor can make, and keeps no database of visitors.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top.
