Cookie policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Semaphore Lab sets no cookies: no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, no third-party cookies and no sign-in cookies.

Optional error reports

Error reports are not cookies, but they are optional, so they are part of the same choice. If you allow them, the page sends a short anonymous report when something breaks. The privacy policy describes exactly what is in one.

Changing your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the main site at any time, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
